# 📌 PROJECT OVERVIEW
TrustShield 254 is an AI-powered cybersecurity platform designed to help Kenyan users identify suspicious online activities before they click links, pay for products, accept job offers, or share personal information. The system combines machine learning, natural language processing (NLP), and community-based reporting to analyze suspicious messages, URLs, job advertisements, and online marketplace listings.
The platform generates risk assessments, highlights potential warning signs, and provides practical safety recommendations. Its community intelligence component identifies repeated indicators across user reports, helping users recognize possible scam patterns that might otherwise go unnoticed.
TrustShield 254 aims to promote digital trust, improve cybersecurity awareness, and contribute to a safer digital environment in Kenya.


# ❗PROBLEM STATEMENT
The growth of digital commerce, online recruitment, and mobile financial services has created more opportunities for cybercriminals to exploit unsuspecting users through phishing, fraudulent job advertisements, fake online sellers, impersonation, and suspicious payment requests.
Many users lack the technical knowledge required to distinguish legitimate digital interactions from potentially fraudulent ones. Furthermore, information about suspicious contacts, websites, and advertisements is often scattered across different platforms, making it difficult to identify recurring scam patterns.
Existing detection tools may focus on specific threats, such as malicious URLs or spam messages, without combining these findings with community-reported information.
Therefore, there is a need for an accessible and intelligent platform that analyzes suspicious digital content, connects related community reports, explains potential risks, and provides appropriate safety recommendations. TrustShield 254 seeks to address this challenge through an integrated AI-powered and community-driven digital trust solution.

# 🎯OBJECTIVES
General Objective
To develop an AI-powered community scam intelligence platform that helps users identify suspicious digital content, recognize recurring scam indicators, and make informed decisions when interacting with online services.
Specific Objectives
1. To develop machine learning models capable of analyzing suspicious messages, URLs, job advertisements, and online marketplace listings.
2. To identify potential phishing attempts, fraudulent recruitment offers, suspicious payment requests, and other common digital scam indicators.
3. To implement a community reporting system that enables users to submit suspicious content and share relevant warning signs.
4. To develop a community intelligence mechanism that identifies repeated indicators across reports and highlights potential relationships between suspicious activities.
5. To generate explainable risk assessments that communicate detected warning signs, uncertainty, and recommended safety actions.
6. To evaluate the performance of the detection models using appropriate machine learning metrics, including precision, recall, F1-score, and confusion matrices.
7. To promote digital safety awareness and responsible online behavior among Kenyan shoppers, job seekers, and mobile-money users.
